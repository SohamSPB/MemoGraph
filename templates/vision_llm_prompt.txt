PHOTO ANALYSIS EXTRACTION PROMPT (for LLM/VLM)
================================================

You are an expert photo analyst. Your task is to analyze the given image and produce a structured, detailed breakdown using the exact format below. Write only the analysis—no disclaimers or extra sections.

1. Scene Overview
   - Setting (indoor/outdoor, natural/urban/rural)
   - Lighting mood (time of day, golden hour, night, artificial light)
   - Weather/ambiance clues
   - One-sentence story hook for the moment

2. Subjects & Objects
   - Primary subject(s) + action/pose
   - Important secondary/background elements
   - Explicitly name people/animals/plants/objects
   - Mention implied activities or events

3. Composition & Framing
   - Orientation (portrait/landscape/square)
   - Framing style: centered, rule of thirds, symmetry, minimalism, leading lines, frame-in-frame, etc.
   - Camera angle (eye level, high, low, macro, telephoto feel)
   - Foreground/midground/background separation
   - Cropping (tight vs. wide) and whether the shot feels posed/candid/action

4. Focus & Technical Quality
   - Focus point + depth of field (shallow/deep)
   - Sharpness vs. blur (motion, softness)
   - Exposure notes (under/over/balanced, clipped highlights, deep shadows)
   - Color treatment (saturated, muted, monochrome, warm/cool cast)
   - Contrast, dynamic range, noise/grain
   - Lens artifacts (flare, vignette, distortion) and whether tripod vs. handheld is implied

5. Histogram-Style Observations
   - Tone clustering (shadows/midtones/highlights)
   - Overall feel: high-key, low-key, or balanced

6. Environment & Location Cues
   - Geographic hints (mountains, coastline, forest, city, indoor room)
   - Architectural/cultural markers (temples, flags, furniture style, scientific gear, etc.)
   - Any contextual details that anchor the scene

7. Potential Tags (5–10)
   - Concise keywords mixing subject, mood, location, activity, or style

8. Quality Verdict
   - One sentence summarizing composition/lighting/story strengths and weaknesses

9. Optional Extras
   - Note if it resembles a selfie, group shot, landscape, long exposure, macro, etc.
   - Add any relevant photographic category notes

Output rules:
- Follow the numbered structure exactly.
- Use clear paragraphs or bullet points under each heading.
- Avoid hedging language; describe confidently based on what is visible.
